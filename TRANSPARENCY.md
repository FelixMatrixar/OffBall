# How OffBall uses AI

OffBall puts AI-written graphics, captions and answers next to a football match. This note says what the AI does, how its work is checked, what it can't do yet, and how to tell us when it gets something wrong (PRD 18.7).

## Everything is synthetic, and labelled

- Every match, club and player is synthetic: generated by our match engine, with fictional names. No real match data is shown.
- Every caption, graphic, answer and export is labelled as AI-generated, and every graphic on the stream carries the labels `synthetic-data` and `ai-generated`.

## What the AI does

A crew of agents, each with one job, built with Microsoft Agent Framework:

| Agent | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Scout | Picks which moments the detectors found are worth airing |
| Explainer | Writes why a moment matters, as separate claims, each with evidence |
| Fact-checker | Checks every claim independently, recomputing every number with the same tools |
| Persona | Writes each viewing mode's words in English, Spanish and French |
| Producer | Times the graphics on the pitch and in the caption bar |
| Pundit | Answers fans' questions in Ask OffBall |
| Engineer | Watches the crew's health and switches on pre-approved fallbacks, such as a faster model |

Code, not AI, does the counting: positions, speeds, distances, space and the moment detectors are computed from the tracking and events, and the agents can only quote numbers those tools return.

## How the work is checked

- **Numbers come from tools.** A claim with a number names the metric and parameters that produced it, so the Fact-checker recomputes it rather than trusting it.
- **A separate checker.** The Fact-checker didn't write the claims it checks. A draft it rejects goes back to the Explainer at most twice; after that, nothing airs.
- **Scripts are checked too.** Before anything airs, the Fact-checker reads every mode's lines in every language and removes any line with a number or fact the verified claims don't support.
- **No fallback text.** If an agent fails, its step airs nothing. There are no templates to fill the gap.
- **Corrections.** When the data feed corrects an event, graphics that cite it are re-checked. If they no longer hold, they're replaced and the old ones are visibly retracted with a Correction tag.
- **People decide.** A producer can approve, hold, skip or retract any broadcast graphic, and can undo the Engineer's changes.
- **Measured.** `offball eval agents` scores each agent on its own task set, including a set of drafts with planted mistakes the Fact-checker must catch (PRD 14.15).

## Known limits

- The agents have so far been tested against a stand-in model in automated tests. Their scores on a real model are published in `calibration/reports/agents.md` once that run is made.
- The synthetic data still misses four of its nine quality targets (realism, physics, behaviour and the discriminator); `DATASHEET.md` gives the numbers.
- Captions are written in English, Spanish and French. The app's other seven languages show the frontend's own reviewed lines, and every language other than English awaits review by native-speaking fans.
- Some analysis isn't built yet: space value, credit for off-ball work, run classification and what-if replays computed live.
- Nothing here is betting, coaching or scouting advice, and nothing describes real teams or players.

## Reporting a problem

If OffBall says something wrong, unfair or inappropriate, open an issue on the repository with the match, the time and the mode you were watching, and what it said. For anything security-related, see `SECURITY.md`.
