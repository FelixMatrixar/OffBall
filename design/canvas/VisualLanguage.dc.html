Overlay grammar

control_field

Team colours over a neutral midpoint, blurred. Contested space also gets dots, so hue is never the only cue.

gap_ring

Lime is reserved for space. One ring at a time; it pulses once on entry.

run_arrow

Solid is the actual run; dashed is planned or counterfactual. Draws slightly ahead of the runner.

pass_line

Line weight carries pass difficulty. Dashed ahead of the ball, solid behind it.

ghost

What would have happened: a dashed outline, never filled, always labelled.

offside_line

Analyst view. The defending team’s last line, with its distance from goal.

player_tag

Text on a halo so it reads over any field colour. Rendered as DOM text in the app.

badge

Threshold badges: sprints above 25.2 km/h and each kilometre covered.

caption

Plain words first; evidence one tap away. Always labelled AI-generated and fact-checked.

correction

A retracted graphic stays visible for 3 s with a Correction tag, then its patch replaces it.

Motion

Enter 200 ms Dwell 2.5 s or longer 150
  • Ease out on entry, ease in on exit. Nothing moves faster than the play it describes.
  • A gap ring pulses once, over 1.6 s. Nothing else loops.
  • Players glide between 25 Hz samples; the renderer never extrapolates more than 200 ms.
  • Reduced motion: fades only, no pulses, no gliding.
