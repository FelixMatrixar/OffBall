Motion and micro-interactions

Every animation explains something: where space opened, what just changed, what the system is doing. Each demo below loops so you can see its timing.

Explain, don’t decorate

Motion shows cause and change. If removing it loses no meaning, remove it.

Faster than the play

UI motion finishes in under 350 ms so it never lags the match. Only drawing on the pitch takes longer.

One thing at a time

On the pitch, one overlay enters at a time; everything else holds still so the eye knows where to go.

Reduced motion is a mode

With reduced motion, changes are instant or fade. No pulses, no gliding, no loops.
